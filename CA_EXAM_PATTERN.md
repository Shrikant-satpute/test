# CA Exam Pattern — New Scheme 2024 (ICAI)
> Complete reference: Stages, Papers, Patterns, SPOM, and Question Styles

---

## Full Journey at a Glance

```
Class 12
   ↓
CA Foundation (4 papers)
   ↓
CA Intermediate — Group 1 & Group 2 (both must be cleared)
   ↓
Articleship — 2 Years
   + SPOM Sets A, B, C, D (cleared during articleship)
   ↓
CA Final — Group 1 & Group 2
   ↓
CA Qualified 🎉
```

## Stage 3: Articleship (2 Years)

> Practical training under a Chartered Accountant. No exams during this period.

### SPOM — Self-Paced Online Modules

Must be completed and cleared **during articleship** before appearing in CA Final.

| Set | Subject | Type |
|-----|---------|------|
| A | Corporate & Economic Laws | Mandatory |
| B | Strategic Cost & Performance Management | Mandatory |
| C | Risk Management **OR** International Taxation (pick one) | Elective |
| D | Psychology **OR** Philosophy **OR** Digital Ecosystems (pick one) | Elective |

**Minimum pass**: 50% in each module test

## SPOM Set A: Corporate & Economic Laws — Detailed Pattern

> The most important SPOM for law students. Covers Companies Act + Securities Laws + Economic Laws.

### Exam Overview

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Total Questions | 50 MCQs |
| Total Marks | 100 (2 marks per question) |
| Question Type | 100% Case Scenario-Based MCQs |
| Compulsory | Yes — all 50 questions, no optional |
| Negative Marking | None |
| Duration | 3 hours (180 minutes) |
| Mode | Computer-Based Test (CBT) at ICAI test centers |
| Portal | spmt.icai.org |
| Result | Immediate — shown on screen after submission |
| Pass Marks | 50/100 (50%) |
| Attempts | Unlimited |
| Re-attempt Fee (India) | ₹500 per attempt |
| Re-attempt Fee (Abroad) | USD 150 per attempt |
| Prerequisite | Complete 40 hours on LMS (lms.icai.org) first |

### Syllabus & Marks Distribution

| Topic | Approx. Marks |
|-------|--------------|
| Companies Act, 2013 | ~50 |
| Securities Laws (SEBI) | ~20 |
| Economic Laws (FEMA + FCRA + IBC) | ~30 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### Question Style — Case Scenario MCQ

Every MCQ is embedded inside a factual legal scenario. A paragraph describes a real-world business/legal situation, followed by 2–5 questions based on it.

**Example:**

> **Case:** XYZ Ltd. is a listed company with a net profit of ₹5 crores. The Board proposes to appoint Mr. A as Managing Director with a remuneration of ₹80 lakhs per annum. However, the company had incurred losses in the previous financial year. A minority shareholder group files a complaint with NCLT alleging oppression by the majority directors and misuse of company funds.
>
> **Q1.** What is the maximum remuneration payable to the MD of XYZ Ltd. without Central Government approval in the given scenario?
> - (a) ₹60 lakhs
> - (b) ₹84 lakhs
> - (c) ₹1.2 crore
> - (d) No ceiling applies
>
> **Q2.** Which authority has jurisdiction to hear the complaint filed by the minority shareholders?
> - (a) Company Law Board
> - (b) NCLT
> - (c) High Court
> - (d) ROC

### Frequently Tested Topics (Based on Student Memory Analysis)

- Managerial remuneration calculations (especially for loss-making companies)
- Director appointment, reappointment, disqualification procedures
- Winding-up provisions and ROC/NCLT powers
- FEMA: employee residential status, property purchase abroad, RBI approval thresholds
- SEBI: penalties, board member appointment authority, insider trading
- IBC: public announcement deadlines, operational vs financial creditor distinction, demand notice timelines
- Secretarial audit thresholds for unlisted companies
