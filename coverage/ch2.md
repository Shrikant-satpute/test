# Ch 2 — Managerial Remuneration (Sec 196–205, Sch V)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 196 | MD/WTD/Manager appointment | Max 5 yr; min 21 / max 70 (SR for 70+); only one of MD/Manager |
| 197 | Overall remuneration limits | 11% aggregate; 5% single MD; 10% multi-MD; 1% non-exec (with MD) / 3% (without); sitting fees excluded |
| 198 | Net profits computation | ≠ P&L profit; non-cash items, capital gains, subsidies, write-backs excluded |
| 200 | CG approval | No longer needed post-2017; SR suffices |
| 201 | SR filing | Filing requirements |
| 202 | Compensation for loss of office | NO compensation if removed by CG / for misconduct |
| 203 | KMPs | CEO/MD, CFO, CS, WTD; same person ≠ MD + CS |
| 204 | Secretarial audit | Listed + unlisted public with paid-up ≥ ₹50 Cr OR turnover ≥ ₹250 Cr |
| Sch V | Remuneration in loss/inadequate-profit yrs | Slabs: <₹5 Cr → ₹30 L; ₹5–100 Cr → ₹42 L; ₹100–250 Cr → ₹60 L; >₹250 Cr → ₹84 L. SR **doubles** limits. |

## Top Traps
- Sec 198 net profits ≠ P&L net profit.
- Sch V slabs use **effective capital** not paid-up.
- Loss yrs → Sch V applies, not Sec 197 %.
- Both 5% (single) and 10% (multi) must fit within 11% aggregate.
- Sitting fees outside Sec 197 cap.
- Manager OR MD, never both.
- 69-yr-old MD reappointed for 5 more yrs (crosses 70) → SR, not CG.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- Loss-making co. retains MD at ₹55 L → which Sch V slab; does SR double it?
- 3 WTDs + 1 non-exec chair → compute max each given profit figure.
- MD removed by CG — claim Sec 202 compensation?
- Listed co. with no MD → does 3% non-exec cap shift?
