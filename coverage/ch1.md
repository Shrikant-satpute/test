# Ch 1 — Appointment & Qualification of Directors (Sec 149–169)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 149 | Board composition, women director, IDs | Min 3 (public) / 2 (private) / 1 (OPC); IDs: 1/3 (non-exec chair) vs 1/2 (exec chair); max 15 needs SR |
| 149(6) | ID criteria | Cannot hold >2% voting power; no material pecuniary relationship in past 2 FYs |
| 149(10)-(11) | ID tenure | 5+5 max = 2 consecutive terms; 3-yr cooling-off; during cooling-off NO role in any capacity |
| 150 | Databank, proficiency test | Min 60%; unlimited attempts |
| 152 | Appointment | 2/3 of public co. directors retire by rotation; Additional Director till next AGM |
| 153 | DIN | Mandatory before appointment; lifetime validity |
| 161 | Additional/alternate/nominee | Alternate cannot be a current director of same co. |
| 164 | Disqualifications | Conviction ≥6 months; 3 consecutive yrs non-filing; 1 yr default on deposits/debentures/dividend |
| 165 | Number of directorships | Max 20 companies; max 10 public |
| 168 | Resignation | Co.: DIR-12 within 30 days; Director: DIR-11 within 30 days |
| 169 | Removal | OR + special notice; director's right to speak at meeting |

## Top Traps
- 182-day resident director = **CALENDAR year** (≠ FEMA 182-day financial year). See cross-law-traps.
- ID count: **"whichever is LESS"** not "more".
- After 2 ID terms: no reappointment even with SR for 3 yrs.
- Sitting fees excluded from 11% managerial-remuneration cap.
- Nominee director is NOT independent.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- Non-exec chair (retired exec) — tests 1/3 vs 1/2 ID threshold.
- ID near end of 2nd term offered "consulting" — does cooling-off bar it?
- Sec 164(2) disqualification in Co. A — ripple effect on other boards?
- 71-yr-old proposed as MD — SR vs CG approval?
