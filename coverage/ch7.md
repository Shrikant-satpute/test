# Ch 7 — Winding Up (Sec 270–310)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 270 | Two modes | Compulsory (Tribunal); Voluntary |
| 271 | Compulsory grounds | **6 grounds**: unable to pay debts (demand >₹1 lakh, 21 days); SR by co.; sovereignty/security; fraudulent/unlawful; 5 consecutive yrs non-filing; just & equitable |
| 272 | Petitioners | Co., creditor, contributory, RoC, authorized person. **RoC cannot petition on grounds (b) and (d)** |
| 274 | On hearing | Dismiss, adjourn, interim, or wind up |
| 275 | Liquidator | NCLT appoints Official Liquidator or IP |
| 302 | Dissolution | NCLT orders after winding up complete |
| 304 | Declaration of solvency (MVWU) | Debts payable within **3 years**; by majority of directors; sworn before Magistrate/Notary |
| 305 | Members meeting (MVWU) | SR for winding up within 5 weeks of Declaration |
| 306 | Creditors meeting (CVWU) | Same day or next day as members meeting |
| 308 | Appointment of liquidator | Members in VWU; creditors in CVWU if no declaration |

## Top Traps
- Inability-to-pay threshold: **₹1 lakh** (Companies Act) vs **₹1 crore** (IBC). See cross-law-traps.
- Non-filing: **5 consecutive years** (Companies Act).
- MVWU needs Declaration of Solvency; else proceeds as CVWU.
- RoC petition: allowed on (a),(c),(e),(f); **barred** on (b) and (d).
- Declaration: debts payable within **3 years** (not 1).
- IBC overrides on inability-to-pay defaults, but Sec 271(e) non-filing still actionable.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- Demand ₹80,000 unpaid 25 days → meets Sec 271(a)? (No — <₹1 L.)
- Declaration filed; auditor later refuses to sign accounts → can MVWU continue?
- RoC files claiming fraud → valid petitioner? (No — Sec 272 bars on ground (b).)
- IBC CIRP + simultaneous Sec 271(e) petition → precedence?
- Declaration: debts payable in 3.5 yrs → valid? (No.)
