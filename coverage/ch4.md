# Ch 4 — Inspection, Inquiry & Investigation (Sec 206–227)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 206 | ROC info | Time as specified in notice; no fixed default |
| 207 | Conduct of inspection | Books retainable 180 days; +180 with authority approval (max 360) |
| 208 | Inspector's report | Admissible as evidence; submitted to CG |
| 209 | Search & seizure | Needs Magistrate order; can seal premises |
| 210 | CG investigation order | **3 triggers**: SR by co.; Tribunal order; CG's own opinion |
| 212 | SFIO investigation | **Exclusive** jurisdiction once assigned; offences cognizable AND non-bailable; arrest without warrant |
| 213 | Tribunal-ordered investigation | Applicants: 100 members OR 1/10 (whichever LESS); ₹25,000 deposit |
| 214 | Security for payment | ₹25,000 |
| 216 | Ownership investigation | CG appoints inspector for beneficial ownership |
| 217 | Inspector's powers | Civil court powers; auditor/banker/legal advisor/employee must cooperate |
| 219 | Related company | Inspector may investigate related cos. simultaneously |
| 223 | Report | Interim + final; CG may share with court |
| 226 | Expenses | Co. pays unless investigation reveals no fraud |
| 227 | Privilege | Legal advisor: **absolute**; Banker: protected UNLESS Tribunal directs |

## Top Traps
- Sec 210 → **3** triggers, not 2 (SR by co. often forgotten).
- SFIO has exclusive jurisdiction; state police/other agencies must stop and hand over.
- Sec 212 offences are cognizable **AND** non-bailable (both, not one).
- Sec 213 threshold same numbers as Sec 244 but different context.
- Document retention max **360 days** total.
- Banker privilege overridable only by **Tribunal** (not CG, not Inspector).
- Legal advisor privilege **absolute** — no override.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- SFIO + state police investigating same person — priority?
- Inspector keeps books 170 days; can he extend without approval?
- Legal advisor and banker both refuse cooperation — different outcomes.
- 100 members vs 95 members holding 15% — who qualifies under Sec 213?
- Search & seizure vs attorney-client privilege claim.
