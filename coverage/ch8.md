# Ch 8 — Foreign Companies (Sec 379–390)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 379 | Application to foreign cos. | Sec 380–386 + 392–393 apply to ALL; full Act applies if Indians hold ≥50% paid-up |
| 380 | Documents to file | Charter, MoA, AoA, directors, principal officer — within **30 days** of establishing place of business |
| 381 | Accounts | Prepare and file accounts in prescribed form |
| 384 | Debentures | Specific debenture rules |
| 386 | "Foreign company" | Incorporated outside India + has place of business in India |

## Top Traps
- Not all of Companies Act applies to foreign cos. — only Sec 380–386 and 392–393, plus full Act if Indians ≥50%.
- "Place of business" = physical presence; online-only ≠ trigger.
- Filing deadline **30 days** (not 60).

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- Foreign co., 40% Indian holding, liaison office — which sections apply?
- Operations stopped but place of business not formally closed — still file accounts?
- US co. opens project office for one infra project — "place of business"?
