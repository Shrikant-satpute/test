# Ch 9 — NCLT, NCLAT & Adjudication (Sec 408–434)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 408 | NCLT constitution | President + Judicial + Technical Members; constituted by CG |
| 410 | NCLAT | Appellate over NCLT |
| 412 | Member qualifications | President: (former) HC judge; Judicial: HC judge or eligible; Technical: IAS-equivalent + 25 yrs expertise |
| 421 | Appeal NCLT → NCLAT | **45 days**; condonable +45 = **max 90** |
| 422 | Expeditious disposal | Endeavour 3 months — not mandatory cap |
| 423 | Appeal NCLAT → SC | **Question of law only** |
| 430 | Civil court bar | No civil court jurisdiction |
| 431 | Contempt | NCLT/NCLAT punish for contempt |
| 434 | Transfer | CLB pending proceedings → NCLT |

## Top Traps
- NCLT appeal lies to **NCLAT** (not High Court).
- NCLAT → SC only on **question of law** (not facts; not mixed).
- 45 + 45 = **90 days max**.
- Sec 422 three-month target is aspirational, not jurisdictional.
- No parallel civil court remedy.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- Party appeals to HC instead of NCLAT — HC jurisdiction?
- "Mixed question of law and fact" appeal to SC — maintainable?
- NCLT decides after 5 months — order valid? (Yes.)
- 50-day delay due to medical emergency — NCLAT can condone how many more?
