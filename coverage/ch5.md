# Ch 5 — Compromises, Arrangements, Amalgamations (Sec 230–237)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 230 | Compromise/arrangement | 3/4 in **value present + voting**; applicants: co./creditor/member/liquidator; 30-day website notice; proxy 48 hrs prior; meeting waiver: 90% affidavit; regulator response: 30 days |
| 230(4) | Objection threshold | Shareholders 10% share cap.; Creditors 5% of outstanding debt |
| 232 | Merger/amalgamation | Supplementary accounts if last FY ended **>6 months** before first meeting; certified copy to RoC within 30 days; transferee CANNOT hold own shares — must cancel; annual compliance within 210 days of FY end |
| 232(6) | Appointed date | Can be retrospective |
| 233 | Fast-track merger | Eligible: small+small; holding+WOS; startup+startup; startup+small. Approval: **90% members (NUMBER)** + 9/10 creditors (VALUE); 21-day notice to creditors; filed with RD; no CG objection in 60 days → registered |
| 234 | Cross-border merger | RBI **+** NCLT approval; specific jurisdictions |
| 235 | Squeeze-out | 90% acceptance → acquire remaining; within 4 months |
| 236 | Minority purchase | 90% already held → compulsory acquisition at valuer's price |

## Top Traps
- Sec 230 threshold: 3/4 of **value** present and voting (not total).
- Meeting waiver: **90% in value via affidavit** (Sec 230(9)) — not 3/4.
- Fast-track: 90% members **in NUMBER**; creditors 9/10 **in VALUE**.
- Sec 235 (90% of those who accepted) ≠ Sec 236 (90% already held).
- Supplementary accounts trigger: **>6 months** since last FY (not 3).
- Transferee shares of transferor MUST be cancelled — no treasury shares.
- Sec 233: no NCLT approval order; only notification; RD registers.
- Cross-border: RBI approval **before** NCLT sanction.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- NBFC + small co. → does Sec 233 apply? (NBFCs excluded.)
- 88% creditor value signs affidavit → waiver valid? (No — needs 90%.)
- Outbound vs inbound cross-border merger — RBI differences.
- 91% accept squeeze-out; 9% strategic — force out? Timeline?
- RoC filing on day 42 — consequence?
- 5-month-old accounts presented as within 6-month window — borderline.
