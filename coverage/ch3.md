# Ch 3 — Board Meetings & Powers (Sec 173–189)

## Must Cover
| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 173 | Board meetings | 4/yr; first within 30 days of incorporation; gap ≤120 days; 7-day notice; OPC/small/dormant: 1 per half-year, gap ≥90 days |
| 174 | Quorum | 1/3 of total OR 2 directors, **whichever HIGHER**; quorum excludes interested directors |
| 175 | Resolution by circulation | Majority of directors entitled to vote; circulate to ALL; **cannot** be used for Sec 179 mandatory-meeting matters |
| 177 | Audit Committee | Min 3; majority IDs; chair = ID |
| 178 | NRC | Non-exec only; majority IDs; chair = ID |
| 179 | Board-only powers | Calls, buyback, securities, borrow, invest — only at board meeting |
| 180 | Restrictions (SR needed) | Sale of whole undertaking; borrowing > paid-up + free reserves (excludes securities premium) |
| 184 | Disclosure of interest | MBP-1 at first board meeting each FY; updated on change |
| 185 | Loans to directors | Prohibition; narrow exception for MD/WTD in ordinary employment course; includes guarantees |
| 186 | Loans/investments by co. | Limit = HIGHER of (60% × paid-up + free reserves + securities premium) OR (100% × free reserves + securities premium); beyond → SR |
| 188 | RPT | Board approval; SR for material thresholds; related party cannot vote |
| 189 | Register of contracts | Maintain register of all contracts where directors interested |

## Top Traps
- Board notice **7 days** (≠ AGM 21 days).
- Board quorum **"HIGHER"** (not lower).
- Adjournment for no quorum → members present at adjourned meeting form quorum.
- Sec 186: take **HIGHER** of two limits (students apply lower).
- Sec 180 borrowing uses paid-up + free reserves **only** (no securities premium).
- Sec 185 covers guarantees, not just loans.

## Hard Scenario Hooks
- 9 directors, 3 interested → does quorum change? (Yes — exclude interested.)
- Borrow ₹120 Cr; paid-up ₹50 Cr, free reserves ₹60 Cr, premium ₹20 Cr → SR needed?
- Small co., 2 directors → meeting frequency exception + quorum?
- NRC chair is MD's close relative → LODR disqualification?
