# Securities Laws Coverage

## SEBI Act 1992

| Sec | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | SEBI functions | Protect investors; develop + regulate market; make regulations |
| 11A | Prospectus regulation | SEBI regulates issue-related matters |
| 11B | Direction power | Issue directions; **disgorgement**; SEBI's strongest civil tool |
| 11C | Investigation | SEBI appoints IA; examination on oath; all must cooperate |
| 12 | Intermediary registration | SEBI can suspend/cancel |
| 15A | No info penalty | ₹1 lakh/day, max ₹1 Cr |
| 15G | Insider trading | **Higher of ₹25 Cr OR 3× profit** |
| 15H | Takeover non-disclosure | **Higher of ₹25 Cr OR 3× profit** |
| 15HA | Fraudulent/UFTP | **Higher of ₹25 Cr OR 3× profit** |
| 15HB | Other contraventions | Up to ₹1 Cr |
| 15I | Adjudicating Officer | Min Division Chief rank; hearing mandatory |
| 24 | Criminal prosecution | Up to 10 yrs / ₹25 Cr / both |

**Traps:** 15G/H/HA = "higher of ₹25 Cr OR 3×" (not "up to ₹25 Cr"); 15A is **per-day** capped at ₹1 Cr; 11B disgorgement is separate from Sec 24 criminal action; AO must be ≥ Division Chief.

## SEBI LODR 2015

| Reg | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | Board composition | 50% non-exec; IDs 1/3 (non-exec chair) / 1/2 (exec chair); ≥4 meetings; gap ≤120 days |
| 18 | Audit Committee | Min 3; majority IDs; chair = ID; quorum: 2 or 1/3 (greater), min 2 IDs |
| 19 | NRC | Min 3; all non-exec; majority IDs; chair = ID |
| 20 | Stakeholders Relationship Committee | Investor grievances |
| 21 | Risk Management Committee | **Top 1000 listed by market cap** — mandatory |
| 23 | RPT | Material = **LOWER of ₹1000 Cr OR 10% consol. turnover**; audit cmte approval; SR for material; related party can't vote |
| 27 | Corporate governance report | 21 days after quarter-end |
| 33 | Financial results | Quarterly: 45 days; Annual: 60 days |
| 34 | Annual report | 21 working days after AGM |

**Traps:** Material RPT uses **LOWER** (students apply higher); Royalty/brand threshold is 5% of turnover (different); Reg 21 = **top 1000 by market cap**, not all listed; 45 vs 60 day reporting often swapped.

## SEBI ICDR 2018

| Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|
| IPO eligibility | 3-yr op. history; net tangible assets ≥ ₹3 Cr each of 3 yrs; pre-tax op. profit ≥ ₹15 Cr in ≥3 of 5 yrs; net worth ≥ ₹1 Cr each yr |
| MPC lock-in | **20% of post-issue capital; 18 months** from allotment |
| Promoter excess lock-in | 6 months |
| Pre-IPO non-promoter | 6 months post-listing |
| Price band | Cap = Floor × 1.2 (NOT Floor + 1.2) |
| Allocation (book-built) | QIB ≥50%; NII ≥15%; RII ≥35% |
| Rights issue | 15–30 days subscription |

**Traps:** Lock-in: old 3 yrs vs new **18 months MPC / 6 months excess**; price band is **multiplicative (×1.2)** not additive; QIB 50% (not reversed with NII).

## SEBI SAST 2011

| Reg | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Open offer trigger | **25%** of shares/voting rights |
| 3(2) | Creeping acquisition | 25–75% range: **+5% per FY (Apr–Mar)** without open offer |
| 4 | Control trigger | Acquiring "control" triggers open offer regardless of % |
| 7 | Open offer size | **Minimum 26%** of total shares |
| 8 | Offer price | Highest of: negotiated; 52-week VWAP; 26-week VWAP; highest price in last 26 weeks |
| 13 | Public announcement | Within **4 working days** of trigger |
| Disclosures | 5% crossing; every 2% change | Within 2 working days to co. + exchange |

**Traps:** Old trigger 15% (1997 code) vs new **25%** (2011); open offer size **26%** (not 20%); creeping 5% **per FY (Apr–Mar)**, not calendar year; no non-compete fee under 2011.

## SEBI PIT 2015

| Reg | Topic | Tricky Points |
|---|---|---|
| 2(g) | Insider | Connected person OR person in possession of UPSI |
| 2(n) | UPSI | Not generally available; materially price-sensitive; e.g. results, dividends, mergers, KMP changes |
| 3 | Communication of UPSI | Only in ordinary course of business |
| 4 | Trading in possession of UPSI | Complete prohibition |
| Trading window | Closure | Mandatorily closed end of quarter until **48 hrs after results declared** |
| Trading plan | Pre-approved | Cooling-off: **120 CALENDAR DAYS** from public disclosure |
| 7 | Initial disclosure | At 5% shareholding/voting |
| 7 | Continual disclosure | Every 2% change after 5% |

**Traps:** Window reopens **48 hrs AFTER RESULTS** (not after board meeting; not 24 hrs); trading plan cooling-off **120 CALENDAR days** (not trading/business); insider includes mere possessor of UPSI even if not "connected".
